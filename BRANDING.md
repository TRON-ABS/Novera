# Novera identity policy

The official Novera project is maintained by **TRON-ABS** at [github.com/TRON-ABS/Novera](https://github.com/TRON-ABS/Novera). Its identity includes the Novera name, cyan hand emblem and project banner. The signature is **Knowledge for everyone.**

## Accurate references and community discussion

You may accurately name Novera to describe its origin, compatibility, a tutorial, a review or your contribution. Screenshots and links may identify the project without suggesting that your work is endorsed. Preserve applicable copyright and license notices.

## Distinguishing independent projects

A fork, modified distribution or independently maintained service must clearly identify its own maintainer and independent status. Do not describe it as an official Novera release or imply endorsement by TRON-ABS without authorization. Use a distinct product identity when presenting a separate product, to avoid confusion with the official project. Accurate statements such as "based on Novera" remain welcome.

The project's name, emblem and banner should not be used to represent a separate project or service as the official Novera project. Requests for endorsement or official identity use should be addressed to TRON-ABS through the [maintainer's GitHub profile](https://github.com/TRON-ABS).

Novera's source remains available under [Apache License 2.0](LICENSE), with the inherited Jan attribution retained. Copying, modifying and redistributing the source remains permitted under that license. Section 6 does not grant trademark permissions, apart from the descriptive uses it specifies.

This identity policy does not revoke copyright permissions already granted, including permissions for branding files previously published under Apache 2.0. It does not claim exclusive ownership of AI-generated artwork or prohibit lawful descriptive use. Any enforceable brand restrictions depend on the rights actually held and applicable law.

No registered-trademark status or clearance of the Novera name is asserted. Availability checks and any registration would be separate steps. This repository policy is not a trademark registration.
