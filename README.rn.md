## Novera 1.0.1

Novera 1.0.1 iraboneka ku mbuga ziri aha hepfo. Gukuraho iyo porogaramu kuri sisitemu yawe canke ukoreshe umurongo wayo wo guhindura. Ivyashizweho biriho birahindurwa iyo ushizeho porogaramu nshasha canke wemeye ivugurura.

- Umutekano: uburenganzira bukomeye bw’imvukira ku mapaji ya kure, kwemeza APIs z’urubuga n’ibikoresho vya server, ububiko bw’ibanga n’ukwimuka birinzwe, gushika ku dosiye/ibinyamakuru bitekanye, gusesangura urupapuro rw’ibarabara no kwubaka inyandiko. Ivyiyumviro vyerekanwa vyarahinduwe; impanuro zisigaye zikurikiranywa ku mugaragaro.
- Ubuzima bwite: imibare na raporo z’amakosa bisaba kwemera gutandukanye kandi biguma bizimye ku buryo busanzwe. Ivyubatswe birimwo inyandiko z'aho umuntu aja. Ivyigwa vy’ubuhinga biracungururwa; abakoresha baguma bagenzura ugukora.
- Moteri yo mu karere: `llama.cpp v0.9.0` (`b12110`, `cd959bae99ff4bed22bef2604f45241c09bbb8c8`). Verisiyo ya moteri n’iy’ikoreshwa biratandukanye. Ivyagezwe vy'intango n'iyandikisha ry'akarorero/iporojekita birakosorwa, harimwo n'ukunanirwa gutangura birimwo `kv_unified`. Ivyiyumviro vy’umuntu ku giti ciwe biriho birazigama.
- Indimi: 24 indimi z'inyongera n'ibindi bihinduka bizana ikigaragaza n'ihuriro rya README ryahinduwe kuri 104, n'imfunguruzo z'ikigaragaza 2,435 ku rutonde. Ivyo bimenyetso ntivyemeza uburyo bwo kwandika canke ugushigikira imvugo mu rurimi rwose.
- **LFM3-Omni-2B Q4_K_M** — Ivyategekanijwe kuri mudasobwa no kuri telefone ngendanwa. Igishushanyo gikomeye c'inyandiko, amashusho n'amajwi, hakoreshejwe ibisabwa `mmproj` dosiye. Gukuraho: ivyerekeye 1.39 GB ku madosiye yose. Hitamwo kugira ngo ukoreshe uburyo bwinshi bwo gukora. Ijwi riranga bivana n’ibarabara n’imoteri. Igihe kirekire gikoresha ubwonko bwinshi.
- **NVIDIA Nemotron-3.5-Bolt-E4B Q4_K_M** — Ubundi buryo bwo gukoresha umwandiko gusa ku biganiro, kwandika, ibikoresho n'inyishu zitunganijwe. Gukuraho: ivyerekeye 6.18 GB. Bisaba kwibuka vyinshi kuruta . LFM3-Omni kandi ntikora amashusho canke amajwi ubwayo. Inyishu zivugwa zikoresha ijwi ry’urutonde. Hitamwo ku bikorwa vy’inyandiko igihe ufise ubwonko buhagije. Suzuma inyishu z’ukuri ukoresheje amasoko.
- Ku 1.0.1, LFM3-Omni-2B Q4_K_M n'ivyo bisabwa `mmproj-LFM3-Omni-2B-Q4_K_M.gguf` ni impanuro mburabuzi ku biro no kuri telefone ngendanwa; NVIDIA Nemotron-3.5-Bolt-E4B Q4_K_M iguma ari ubundi buryo bwo gukoresha umwandiko gusa. Amahitamwo y’akarorero ariho n’imiterere y’ibintu birazigama gushika bihinduwe mu buryo butomoye.
- Ijwi: injiza y'imikoro yo mu karere ku LFM3-Omni, ikiyago gikomeza n'ugutuma kwihuta, amajwi y'inyishu y'abagabo/abagore ku bigereranyo vy'amajwi, n'imvugo ya sisitemu ku bigereranyo vya kera. Igipande kimwe, uburyo butanu: **Ivyikoresho / Umwandiko / Ishusho / Umuziki / Amasanamu**. Igenzura rikurikira uburyo bwatowe: igipimo, umuti, igihe, urugero, uburyo, ibikoresho, amajambo, imbuto n’ingero. Ubwaguke bw'urubaho n'amabuto bihuye n'idirisha n'ibimenyetso vyahinduwe.

- **1–10 uruvyaro rukurikiranye**, mbere na mbere kuri 1, n'ingero zigenga z'ishusho, umuziki na videwo. Guhagarika gukuraho igikorwa gikora n'umurongo usigaye.
- **Imbuto zidasanzwe, zidahinduka, +1 na −1**, ibiharuro vyategekanijwe canke bigenewe kandi bidakenewe **Real-ESRGAN ×2/×4** gutera imbere n'ingero za nyuma zigaragazwa.
- Icogereranyo catowe, ubwonko bwikora, ibifatanijwe, amabwirizwa, ubushakashatsi ku rubuga n’ibikoresho biguma mu murongo umwe. Ibikoresho bivana n’ubushobozi bw’akarorero n’uburenganzira bwo kubironka.

## Kuva ku gutangura kwa mbere gushika ku bisubizo

**Ubuhinga bwacu bwo kwikoresha buhuza ugutegura n’ugushitsa igikorwa cose.** Novera ifata intambwe z'ubuhinga ziboneka ku miterere yatowe, mu gihe igumya ubugenzuzi buteye imbere bushikira.

| Intambwe | Ivyo Novera ikora |
| --- | --- |
| **1. Gutangura** | Hitamwo ururimi rwa sisitemu rushigikiwe mu gutangura ubwa mbere maze ukabika ivyo ukunda. Itanga ivyitegererezo vy’intango n’amakuru ngirakamaro y’ibikoresho. |
| **2. Gutegura** | Gushiramwo ibintu birongowe bitanga inyandiko, ivy’ugushiramwo, ishusho, umuziki, amasanamu n’ugutera imbere. Iraba ibice, ubunini n'umwanya w'ubuntu imbere y'uko wemeza, hanyuma ikurikirana ivyo washizeko n'iterambere, ihagarika/isubiramwo n'igenzura rya SHA-256. |
| **3. Gutunganya** | Yandika ibigereranyo n’ibice vyavyo, ategura imirongo y’ivyo bihuye n’ivyo bisabwa. Gusubiramwo ivy'imbere bitangura kuri CPU n'ihitamwo ryabitswe CPU/GPU. Imirongo mishasha y'umuziki ihinduka uburyo bw'ibikoresho. |
| **4. Gutahura ivyo usavye** | Inzira z’imodoka zemeye ibisabwa vyo kurema ku nyandiko, ishusho, umuziki canke amasanamu. Ushobora kandi guhitamwo neza uburyo bwo gusohoka n'ikigereranyo c'aho hantu canke c'igicu. |
| **5. Gushitsa** | Ukwibuka kwikora kurindira igikorwa gikora, kurekura ibigereranyo bidakenewe kandi gusubira gushiramwo ibikoresho bikenewe. Urutonde rw'ibikorwa 1–10 rugenda mu buryo butegekanijwe kandi rurekura ibikoresho imbere y'akazi gakurikira. Abatanga igicu bakoresha konti yatowe n’imiterere. |
| **6. Kugarura no gusubira gukoresha** | Ibika ibinyamakuru muri Novera amadosiye ya sisitemu, yerekana ibisubizo n'ibihe biriho, hanyuma itanga ibihinduka, ububiko bw'ibitabu, gukopa, kwohereza hanze canke gusangira sisitemu. Kurungika ku yindi porogarama biguma ari igikorwa cawe. |

Ukwikoresha bipfutse moteri zihurikiye hamwe n’imigenderanire y’ibikorwa; ntibituma umuderi wose wa Hub ushobora gukorwa. Ivyuma, ibice vyashizwemwo n’uburyo ababitanga nivyo bigena ivyo bishobora gukora. Amadosiye, amahinduka ya dosiye n'amabwirizwa y'igikoko bikurikira uburenganzira bwo gushika ku kiganiro.

[Inyobora y'ukoresha](docs/USER_GUIDE.md) · [Moteri n'ukwemeza](docs/SUIVI_MOTEURS.md) · [Iterambere](docs/DEVELOPMENT.md)

Ku 1.0.1, LFM3-Omni-2B Q4_K_M n'ivyo bisabwa `mmproj-LFM3-Omni-2B-Q4_K_M.gguf` ni impanuro mburabuzi ku biro no kuri telefone ngendanwa; NVIDIA Nemotron-3.5-Bolt-E4B Q4_K_M iguma ari ubundi buryo bwo gukoresha umwandiko gusa. Ivya mbere ni 32,768 ibimenyetso ku biro na 2,048 kuri telefone ngendanwa. Ivyiyumviro birebire bikoresha ubwonko bwinshi. Amahitamwo y’akarorero ariho n’imiterere y’ibintu birazigama gushika bihinduwe mu buryo butomoye.

## Ubushobozi buri mu isoko

Ivy'ugucungera no gutegura ubuzima bwite vyateguwe kuri 1.0.1. Imibare y'amahitamwo n'ugutanga raporo y'amakosa bikoresha ukwemera kwigenga, gutomoye n'isuzuma ry'ubuhinga ryayunguruwe. Ivyiyumviro vy’ugusohoka bisabwa n’ibindi bimenyetso vy’umutekano bisigaye vyanditswe ukwavyo. [igitabo co gusanasana](docs/MAINTENANCE.md)

| Uburebure | Ibirango |
| --- | --- |
| **Ivyanditswe n'iyerekwa** | Ikiyago, inyandiko zifatanye n’ibindi, inyishu zica ku rubuga n’ibigereranyo vyo mu karere canke vyo mu gicu. Iyerekwa rivana n’akarorero; gutahura no gutanga amashusho ni ubushobozi butandukanye. |
| **Ishusho** | Mu kiganiro, imbuto, imigereranyo, umuti, ibice vya GGUF/Safetensors, amadosiye ya Diffusers yatunganijwe n’umurongo wa NVFP4 CUDA ushigikiwe. |
| **Muzika** | Guhingura ibintu mu karere canke mu gicu, igihe bimara, uburyo, ibikoresho canke amajwi aho bishigikiwe, amajambo atunganijwe n’igikoresho co gukina amajwi. Amahitamwo y’abatanga aratandukanye. |
| **Videwo** | Ivyakozwe mu karere canke mu gicu, igihe, igiharuro c’amafoto/cadence y’imirongo y’aho hantu, imigereranyo, ubushobozi n’igikoresho co gukina amasanamu. |
| **Gushaka n'ibikoresho** | Gushaka ku rubuga, gushaka inyandiko zishingiye ku gushiramwo, MCP, ibibanza vyo gukoreramwo n’ibikoresho vyo mu karere hakurikijwe infashanyo y’akarorero n’uburenganzira bwo kuronka. |
| **Ivyavuyemwo n'ugusangira** | Isoko ry’ibinyamakuru, ibihinduka, inyishu/ibiganiro vyoherezwa hanze, inyandiko n’ibinyamakuru vy’umwimerere, gucapura/PDF n’ugusangira uburyo bushingiye ku rubuga. |
| **Gucungera umutungo** | Ukwibuka kwikora gukoreshwa ku buryo busanzwe, CPU/GPU imiterere y'ibice bishigikiwe n'imirongo y'amaboko iyo ibikoresho vyemeza. |

| **Ishusho — uburyo bwo gukoresha** | **FLUX.2 Klein 4B NVFP4**, biciye mu nzira y'akavukire CUDA igihe GPU n'ibidukikije bihuye. |

## Hub n'ibigereranyo bihuye

**GGUF, gisanzwe n'igitigiri Safetensors, ONNX, na MLX**: gutohoza ibigereranyo ku gikorwa maze uhitemwo ibihinduka bihuye na moteri n’ibikoresho vyawe.

| Umuryango | Gushigikira mu isoko | Ibisabwa |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **GGUF** | Umwandiko biciye muri llama.cpp; ishusho/ibice vya videwo vy'imirongo ishigikiwe. | Ubwubatsi bwemewe na moteri ifatanye. |
| **Gucapura GGUF** | Ihinduka rimwe ku rutonde rwuzuye, ubunini bushizwe hamwe, gukuraho ibice vyose n'ubunini/SHA-256/igenzura ry'umutwe. | Amazina y’amadosiye afise inomero zisanzwe; gukuraho 500 GB ntibisigura ko ikigereranyo gihuye n'iki PC. |
| **Itegeko Safetensors** | Uzuza amadosiye biciye muri Transformers, umwandiko w'impamvu kuri CPU canke NVIDIA CUDA. | Gutunganya, gutera ikimenyetso n’igice cose gikenewe. |
| **ONNX Runtime** | Ukwinjiza mu gihugu kw'ibishushanyo vy'imibare/ibiharuro vy'imibare n'ivyoherezwa hanze vy'inyandiko vyateguwe GenAI. | Dosiye yuzuye yemejwe; igikorwa-kihariye co gutegura imbere/inyuma y’ugutegura bisabwa; [gutegura n'imipaka](onnx-server/README.md). |
| **LoRA / PEFT** | Injira urufatiro rwuzuye n'umurongozi warwo witandukanye mu mutanga Safetensors. | Ishingiro ry’inkomoko Transformers ritagira igitigiri, akaranga gahuye, igenzura ry’igihe n’ingereranyo y’ukwibuka kw’urugero; [gutegura n'imipaka](transformers-server/README.md#local-lora--peft-adapters). |

| **NVFP4 LLM / ModelOpt** | Igipande-16 FP4 ivyerekanywe biciye mu GEMMs z'imvukira CUDA; itandukanye n'ishusho NVFP4. | GPU ya Blackwell ihuye, ishigikiwe n’ingero/ugushora hanze n’itohoza ry’ukuri ry’umubiri; ibigereranyo vy'igerageza bitamenyerejwe vyageragejwe kuri RTX 5070. |

| **EXL2** | Igikoresho co gushiramwo ExLlamaV2; guca, gukuraho no gukuraho vyageragejwe kuri GPU. | Hitamwo neza CUDA Ivyiyumviro vy'inyuma vy'ikirundo ca porogaramu yageragejwe; ikigereranyo c'igenzura cuzuye n'ibihagije VRAM birakenewe. |

Igihugu ubu kirafata **LFM3-1.7B** nk'impanuro yayo LiquidAI ku miterere ihuye, n'ibihinduka Q4_K_M, Q6_K na Q8_0. Icogereranyo ca mbere kiguma ari co kiri ku rutonde rw’imiterere iyobowe iri hejuru.

Icungera uburemere bwo gukuraho **munsi ya 1 GB gushika kuri 500 GB no hejuru**, hanyuma ushire mu migwi hakurikijwe ibikoresho bihuye, ubunini, ubushasha canke ivyo ushobora gukuraho. Ivyavuyemwo biratera imbere buhoro buhoro; amayunguruzo akoreshwa ku bigereranyo vyazanywe n'uburyo bwavyo mburabuzi. Impanuro z'ukwibuka zikoresha RAM/VRAM zihari, ibice vy'imirongo n'ivy'abagenzi, ata guharura kabiri kw'ukwibuka kwasangiwe GPU.

AWQ 4-bike, GPTQ 4-bike, NF4 kandi INT8 umwandiko wose wavutse ku RTX 5070 Laptop gukoresha utugereranyo dutoduto tutamenyerejwe twaremwe hanze y’umurongo. Ivyo vyemeza gushiramwo n’ugushirwa mu ngiro, si uburyo canke ubushobozi bw’ikigereranyo cose. **Ivyo vyongeweko biri mu ntango: gusubira kwubaka n’ibishingiye ku bihuye birakenewe; iyi README ivugurura ntiyivugurura ibiharuro bibiri vyashizweho.**

**Bipimwa kuri RTX 5070 Laptop:** Qwen2.5-0.5B-Instruct, ikibanza 2,048, gusaba 272 ibimenyetso, 128 ibimenyetso vyavutse, ibikorwa bitanu bishushe: gusobanura hagati **GGUF Q4_K_M 402.7 tokens/s · EXL3 4 bpw 312.5 · EXL2 4 bpw 146.9**. Nta nyungu y'umuvuduko kurusha GGUF yerekanwe hano. Igitigiri/ugupima biratandukanye, kandi EXL3 yananiwe gusuzuma imibare y'ishimikiro; ivyo bisubizo si umuvuduko rusangi canke urutonde rw’uburyo. [Ingero zuzuye n'imipaka](deliverables/exllama-2026-10-30/validation.md).

**TabbyAPI** ni uburyo bwo guhitamwo, bucungiwe bitandukanye OpenAI-bujanye n'ihuriro rya server, ryazimye ku buryo busanzwe. Ivyemejwe EXL3 ivyerekanwa vy'ukuvumbura n'ugutangaza vyageragejwe. Inzira y'aho hantu ExLlama icungiwe iguma itandukanye. [ExLlama gutegura no guhuza TabbyAPI](transformers-server/README.md#exl3-first-exl2-second--nvidia-execution).

Uburyo busanzwe buhuye n'ibikoresho bukunda ibisohoka bishasha mu rwego rumwe rw'uguhuza n'ubwonko buboneka. Ivyitegererezo vyashizweho ntibisubirizwa ubwavyo.

[Igitigiri Safetensors gutegura](transformers-server/README.md#quantized-safetensors--source-integration-29-october-2026) · [Imiterere, ivy'imbere n'ivyemezo](docs/SUIVI_MOTEURS.md)

Amasoko yahinduwe yongerako **ibice bicungiwe vy'amahitamwo mu miterere ya Windows x64**: ibidukikije vy'ibanga vya Python, imirongo ya CPU/CUDA, ingereranyo n'ukwemeza imbere yo gukuraho, kugenzura, gusanura no gukuraho. Inzira z'umusemuzi zisanzwe ziguma munsi y'amahitamwo ateye imbere. Moteri z'imvukira GGUF zigumya ibice vyazo vya kera; telefone ngendanwa n'igicu ntibishobora gukuraho Python/PyTorch. Ivyiyumviro bitatu vy'ibanga CPU/vyoherezwa hanze vyageragejwe hakoreshejwe amakuru y'igerageza y'ubuhinga; umucungerezi atanga raporo y'ikoreshwa ryiza ry'ibiro ku 1 Ugushyingo 2026; GPU imirongo n'isuku-PC gushirwaho biracari bikeneye kwemezwa bitandukanye, mu buryo burambuye. ## Gufasha abantu, mu rwego rwose

Uretse ishure n’uburezi, icipfuzo cacu ni ugufasha abantu kwiga, gutorera umuti ingorane, guhingura no gutegura imigambi y’ingirakamaro kw’isi yose. Novera igamije gutuma ivy'ubu AI vyoroha gutahura no gukoresha, mu ndimi zose, mu nzego z'ubumenyi n'ibikoresho biriho.

| --- | --- |

| Ubuhinzi, ivyokurya n'imibano yo mu misozi | Gusangira ubumenyi ngirakamaro, gushigikira igenamigambi no gutegura ibikoresho vy’ingirakamaro vyo mu karere. |
