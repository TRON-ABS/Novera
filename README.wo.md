## Novera 1.0.1

Novera 1.0.1 am na ci platform yiñ lim ci suuf. Yebbi kofre bi ngir sa sistem wala nga jëfandikoo chaine yeesal biñ siñe. Instalaasioŋ yi fi nekk dañuy yeesal soo sampalee kofre bu bees bi wala nga nangu yeesal bi.

- Kaaraange: sañ-sañ yu gëna tar ngir xët yu sori, xamle API reso bi ak jumtukaayi serwër yi, dencukaay sekkere ak migraasioŋ buñ aar, jëfandikoo fichier/media bu gëna wóor, jàngat këyitu xayma ak defar script. Dependence yiñ wane yeesal nañu leen; dessitu advisories yi ñu ngi leen di topp ci lu leer.
- Sutura: lim yi ak rapoor njuumte yi dañuy laaj ndigalu wuute te dañuy des ci default. Tabax yi dañuy àndaale ak tabb jëmmal buñ bind. Diagnostic teknik dañu koy segg; jëfandikukat yi ñooy saytu aktivaasioŋ bi.
- Motër lokaal: `llama.cpp v0.9.0` (`b12110`, `cd959bae99ff4bed22bef2604f45241c09bbb8c8`). Versioŋu motër bi ak xeetu aplikaasioŋ bi dañu wuute. Jekkal model bi njëkk ak bindu model/projecteur bi dañu leen saafara, boole ci jafe-jafe ndoorte li ci `kv_unified`. Jekkal sa bopp yi fi nekk dañu leen di denc.
- Làkk yi: 24 yeneen làkk ak xeeñ yi indi interfaas bi ak dajale README biñ tekki ci 104, ak 2,435 caabi interfaas bu nekk ci katalog bu nekk. Lii ñuy wax du seede kalite editorial wala ndimmbalu wax ci làkk wu nekk.
- **LFM3-Omni-2B Q4_K_M** — Defa am ci ordinatër ak mobile. Benn xeetu mbind, nataal ak audio bu dëgër, di jëfandikoo dencukaay biñ soxla `mmproj`. Yebbi: lu tollu ci 1.39 GB ngir ñaari dencukaay yi. Tannal ko ngir am tabb multimodal bu gëna woyof. Man-mani baat mingi aju ci platform bi ak motër bi. Kontekst bu gëna gudd dafay jëfandikoo memory bu gëna bari.

## Liko dalee ci ubbite bu njëkk bi ba ci njariñ yi

**Sunu otomatisation dafay boole waajal ak def liggéey bi yépp.** Novera mooy jëfandikoo jéego teknik yi jàppandi ci tabb biñ tànn, boole ci tëye doxalal yu gëna xarañ yi.

| Jéego | Li Novera di liggéey |
| --- | --- |
| **1. Tàmbali** | Tannal làkku sistem biy jàppale ci ubbite bu njëkk bi ba noppi denc li nga taamu. Dafay wane xeetu ndoorte ak leeral ci hardware yu am njariñ. |
| **2. Waajal** | Instalaasioŋ buñ teg loxo dafay joxe mbind, dugal, nataal, music, wideo ak yokkute. Dafay xoolaat komponent yi, dayo yi ak bayaal bi laata ngay nangu, ba noppi topp yebbite yi ak yokkute, noppalu / dellu ak SHA-256 xool. |
| **3. Tabbal** | Dafay bind ay model ak seeni cër, defar ay profil yu jëm ci loolu ak rapoor ci dependency yiñ soxla. Embeddings yiy jël dañuy tàmbali ci CPU ak tànneef CPU/GPU buñ denc. Profil music yu bees yi dañuy nekk ci mode instrumental. |
| **4. Xam li ñuy laaj** | Yooni oto yi xàmmee nañu sàq yiñ def ci mbind, nataal, music wala wideo. Mën nga itam tànnee ci anam wu leer anam wi ñuy génne ak xeetu lokaal wala cloud. |
| **5. Def** | Memoire automatik dafay xaar liggéey bu am solo, génne model yuñ soxlawul ba noppi sarsewaat jumtukaay yiñ soxla. 1–10 liggéey dañuy dox ci yoon wi, ba noppi bàyyi jumtukaay yi laataa liggéey bi ci topp. Fournisseur cloud yi dañuy jëfandikoo kontu bi nga tànn ak tabb gi. |
| **6. Delloosi ko jëfandikoowaat** | Dafay denc media yi ci dosiye sistem Novera, wane njariñ yi ak waxtu yi jàppandi, ba noppi joxe ay variant, bibliotek bi, kopie, yóbbu wala séddoo sistem. Yonnee ci beneen aplikaasioŋ mooy sa jëf. |

Otomatisation dafay muur motëri liggéey yuñ boole; du tax model Hub bu nekk mën nañu ko jëfandikoo. Hardware bi, composant yiñ samp ak yelleefu ki leen jox ñooy wane li mëna dox. Dosie yi, coppite yi ci fichier yi ak ndigalu shell yi dañu topp sañ-sañu jëfandikoo waxtaan wi.

## Hub ak model yu méngoo

**GGUF, Safetensors standard ak Safetensors yi ñu quantifier, ONNX ak MLX**: xool model yi ci liggéey bi nga tànnee variant yi méngoo ak motër bi ak sa aparey.

| Famille | Jàppale ci balluwaay bi | Liñu lay laaj |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **GGUF** | Defar mbind ci llama.cpp; nataal ak wideo ngir profil yiñ jàppale. | Architecture buñu xàmmee ci motër biñ boole. |
| **Xaaj GGUF** | Benn variant ci série bu mat, dayo buñ boole, yebbi bépp-shard ak dayo / SHA-256 / saytu boppu. | Tur fichier yu am nimero buñ miin; Yebbi 500 GB tekkiwul ni model bi méngoo na ak PC bii. |
| **Safetensors standard** | Dossier yu matt yi jaaraleko ci Transformers, bind sabab yi ci CPU wala NVIDIA CUDA. | Tabluwaay, tokeniseer ak bépp shard buñ soxla. |
| **ONNX Runtime** | Yobbu ci dëkk bi ay graf numérique/bool ak génne mbind GenAI yuñ waajal. | Dossier bu mat te baax; liggéey-jëfandikoo/jëfandikoo giñ soxla; [tabb ak àpp](onnx-server/README.md). |
| **Adaptatëru LoRA/PEFT** | Dugalal benn base bu mat ak adaptëram bu nekk ci Safetensors biy joxe. | Base Transformers buñu xaymawul, dàntite bu méngoo, saytu tensor ak xayma mémoire cumulatif; [tabb ak àpp](transformers-server/README.md#local-lora--peft-adapters). |
| **FP8 LLM / ModelOpt** | Liggéeyukaay CUDA ngir génne Llama/Qwen2/Qwen3 bu dëgër tensor FP8. | Saytu arsitektir/tensor ak sond kernel dëgg; [tabb ak àpp](transformers-server/README.md#modelopt-fp8-and-nvfp4-llms). |
| **NVFP4 LLM / ModelOpt** | Projeksioŋ FP4 yuñ defaree blok-16 jaaraleko ci GEMM yu CUDA; tàqaloo ak nataal NVFP4. | GPU Blackwell bu méngoo, di jàppale dimension/export ak sonde kernel bi; jumtukaay yuñ tàggatul ñu natt leen ci RTX 5070. |
| **AWQ** | 4-bit GEMM xeetu barabu saytu bu amul fusion liggéey. | NVIDIA CUDA ak GPTQModel/Transformers yuñ mëna tànn. |
| **GPTQ** | Tabbu 2/3/4/8 bit, jëmmal gptq/gptq_v2. | NVIDIA CUDA, GPTQModel ak Optimum; jëfandikoo dëgg lañu natt ci 4 bits. |
| **bitsandbytes** | NF4/FP4 wala INT8 8 bit. | NVIDIA CUDA ak ay lëkkaloo yuñ mëna tànn; test NF4 ak INT8 dëgg. |
| **EXL3** | Dossier modelu Safetensors yiñ sarse jaaraleko ci ExLlamaV3; jamono ju dëggu ak CUDA lañu natt. | GPU NVIDIA, environmaa Python 3.12 bu wuute ak tabax bu méngoo; amul CPU buy dellu ginaaw. |
| **EXL2** | ExLlamaV2 chargeur buñ jagleel; di dawal, fomm ak desarge natt ci GPU. | CUDA dañu wara tànn bu baax ngir stack losisel biñ natt; ab model bu matt ak VRAM lañu soxla. |
| **MLX** | Modèle yu méngoo ci Apple Silicon. | Motër MLX ak platform buñ jàppale. |

Hub bi leegi dafay pin **LFM3-1.7B** ni ko LiquidAI digle ngir tabb yi méngoo, ak Q4_K_M, Q6_K ak Q8_0. Modèle biñ njëkka def mooy model biñ lim ci configuration biñ teg ci kaw.

Seggal yebbi poid yi ci **ci suufu 1 GB ba 500 GB ak ci kaw**, ba noppi nga tànnal ko ci jumtukaay bi, dayo bi, bees bi wala yebbite yi. Resultaa yi dañuy yokk ndànk-ndànk; filtre yi dañuy jëfandikoo ci model yiñ jëlee ak seeni variant yiñ jagleel. Xelal ci mémoire dafay jëfandikoo RAM/VRAM bi jàppandi, contexte ak composant àndandoo, te duñu lim ñaari yoon mémoire GPU biñ bokk.

AWQ 4-bit, GPTQ 4-bit, NF4 ak INT8 ku nekk ci ñoom defar na mbind ci RTX 5070 Laptop di jëfandikoo ay model yu ndaw yuñ tàggatul yuñ defar ci offline. Loolu dafay saytu loading ak exécution, du kalite wala performance model bu nekk. **Yokk yooyu ñu ngi ci source bi: tabaxaat ak dependency yu méngoo lañu soxla; yeesal bii README yeesalul ab binar buñ samp.**

**Natt ci RTX 5070 Laptop:** Qwen2.5-0.5B-Instruct, context 2,048, laaj 272 jeton, 128 jeton yuñ defar, juróomi daw yu tàng: dekode median **GGUF Q4_K_M 402.7 tokens/s · EXL3 4 bpw 312.5 · EXL2 4 bpw 146.9**. Amul benn njariñu gaawaay ci kaw GGUF buñu wane fii. Kantite/kalibraasioŋ wuute na, te EXL3 daa lajj ci benn saxaaru arithmetik; Resultaa yi bokku ñu ci rang gaawaay wala kalite. [Natt yu mat sëkk ak àpp](deliverables/exllama-2026-10-30/validation.md).

**TabbyAPI** lëkkalekaay serwër bu méngoo ak OpenAI, buñ mëna tànn, buñ tànn diko yor, te dafa dellu ginaaw. Gis-gis model EXL3 biñ gëm ak streaming lañu natt. Yoonu ExLlama biñ yor ci dëkk bi mingi wéy di wuute. [Tabb ExLlama ak lëkkaloo TabbyAPI](transformers-server/README.md#exl3-first-exl2-second--nvidia-execution).

Xeetu tànneef biñ jagleel hardware bi mooy gëna bëgg génne yu bees yi ci biir benn dëppoo ak mémoire disponible. Modèle yiñ samp duñu leen wecci ci saasi.

[Tabb Safetensors yi ñu quantifier](transformers-server/README.md#quantized-safetensors--source-integration-29-october-2026) · [Format yi, njëkkante yi ak gëmloo](docs/SUIVI_MOTEURS.md)

Sources yu bees yi yokk **komponent yiñ yor ci tànneef yi ngir Windows x64**: environmaa yi Python privé, profil CPU/CUDA yuñ tënk, xayma ak firnde laataa ñuy yebbi, xool, defar ak dindi. Yooni tekkikatu làkk yiñ personaalise dañuy des ci tànneef yu xóot yi. Motëri GGUF yi dañuy tëye seen cër yi fi nekk; mobile ak cloud duñu yebbi Python/PyTorch. Ñatti profil CPU/export yuñ jëfandikoo ak jumtukaayi synthetik dëgg; Toppatookat bi dafa wax ni jëfandikoo na bu baax ordinatër bi ci 1 nowàmbar 2026; Profil GPU ak instalaasioŋ bu sell-PC ba leegi dañu soxla tàqaloo, gëna leer. [Profil yi ak àppu test yi](docs/REPRISE_COMPOSANTS_GERES_80_LANGUES.md). dugal stack bi ci EXE defa yeex.
