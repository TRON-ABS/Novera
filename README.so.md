## Novera 1.0.1

Novera 1.0.1 ayaa diyaar u ah aaladaha hoos ku taxan. Soo deji xirmada nidaamkaaga ama isticmaal kanaalka cusboonaysiinta ee saxeexan. Rakibaadaha hadda jira ayaa cusbooneysiiya marka aad rakibto xirmada cusub ama aad aqbasho cusboonaysiinta.

- Ammaanka: oggolaanshaha adag ee waddaniga ah ee boggaga fogfog, xaqiijinta shabakadda API-yada iyo agabka server-ka, kaydinta sirta ah ee la ilaaliyo iyo socdaalka, faylalka badbaadada/helitaanka warbaahinta, falanqaynta xaashida iyo dhis qoraallada. Ku-tiirsanaanta bannaanka waa la cusboonaysiiyay; talooyinka soo haray si furan ayaa loola socdaa.

- Matoorka maxaliga ah: `llama.cpp v0.9.0` (`b12110`, `cd959bae99ff4bed22bef2604f45241c09bbb8c8`). Nooca injiinka iyo nooca codsigu waa kala duwan yihiin. Dejinta moodeelka hore iyo diiwaan gelinta moodeelka/projector-ka waa la saxay, oo ay ku jirto bilawga fashilka ku lug leh `kv_unified`. Goobaha gaarka ah ee jira waa la xafiday.

- **LFM3-Omni-2B Q4_K_M** — Default on desktop iyo mobile. ## Hub iyo moodooyinka ku habboon

**GGUF, heerka iyo tirada Safetensors, ONNX, iyo MLX**: ku baadho moodooyinka hawl ahaan oo dooro noocyo ku habboon mishiinka iyo qalabkaaga.

| Qoyska | Taageerada isha | Shuruudaha |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **GGUF** | Jiilka qoraalka ilaa llama.cpp; qaybaha sawirka iyo muuqaalka ee profiles la taageeray. | Naqshad uu aqoonsaday matoorka la isku daray. |
| **Kala baxa GGUF** | Hal nooc oo taxane ah oo dhammaystiran, cabbir la soo koobay, dhammaan-shard soo dejineed iyo cabbir/SHA-256/baaritaannada madaxa. | Magacyada caadiga ah ee nambarada leh; soo dejinta 500 GB macnaheedu maaha in moodelku ku habboon yahay kan PC. |
| **Heerka Safetensors** | Ku buuxi faylalka Transformers, qoraalka sababta CPU ama NVIDIA CUDA. | Habaynta, tokenizer iyo jeex kasta oo loo baahan yahay. |
| **ONNX Runtime** | Soo dejinta maxalliga ah ee garaafyada nambarada/bool oo la diyaariyey GenAI dhoofinta qoraalka ah. | Galka dhamaystiran ee la ansixiyay; Hawsha-gaar-gaar-u-samaynta/ka-hortagga ka-hortagga ayaa loo baahan yahay; [dejinta iyo xadka](onnx-server/README.md). |
| **LoRA / PEFT adabtarada** | Soo deji saldhig dhamaystiran oo leh adabtarro maxalli ah oo gooni ah oo ku jira Safetensors bixiyaha. | Sababta aan la qiyaasi karin Transformers saldhigga, aqoonsiga iswaafajinta, jeegaga teensorka iyo qiyaasta xusuusta wadarta; [dejinta iyo xadka](transformers-server/README.md#local-lora--peft-adapters). |
| **FP8 LLM / ModelOpt** | Dhaladka CUDA fulinta ee cufan Llama/Qwen2/Qwen3 per-tensor FP8 dhoofinta | Hubinta dhismaha/tensor iyo baaritaanka kernel dhabta ah; [dejinta iyo xadka](transformers-server/README.md#modelopt-fp8-and-nvfp4-llms). |
| **NVFP4 LLM / ModelOpt** | Baakad-16 FP4 saadaasha ilaa hooyo CUDA GEMMs; ka soocan sawirka NVFP4. | Ku habboon Blackwell GPU, cabbirada la taageeray iyo dhoofinta kernel-ka dhabta ah; moodallo tijaabo oo aan la tababarin ayaa lagu tijaabiyay RTX 5070. |
| **AWQ** | 4-bit GEMM isbaarooyinka moodeelka oo aan la isku darin. | NVIDIA CUDA iyo GPTQModel/Transformers raridayaal ah. |
| **GPTQ** | Habaynta 2/3/4/8-bit, qaab-dhismeedka gptq/gptq_v2. | NVIDIA CUDA, GPTQModel iyo Optimum; fulinta dhabta ah lagu tijaabiyay 4 bits. |
| **bitsandbytes** | 4-bit NF4/FP4 ama 8-bit INT8. | NVIDIA CUDA iyo ku tiirsanaanta ikhtiyaariga ah; dhabta ah NF4 iyo INT8 imtixaanada. |
| **EXL3** | Buuxi tirada Safetensors faylalka moodeelka ah ee lagu shubay ExLlamaV3; Jiilka dhabta ah ee leh CUDA waa la tijaabiyay. | NVIDIA GPU, kala Python 3.12 deegaan iyo qaab dhismeedka ku haboon; Maya CPU dib u dhac. |
| **EXL2** | u go'ay ExLlamaV2 xamuulka; daawashada, baabiinta iyo soo dejinta ayaa lagu tijaabiyay GPU. | CUDA fiiro gaar ah waa in si cad loogu xusho kaydka software-ka ee la tijaabiyay; isbaarada nooca oo dhamaystiran iyo VRAM ayaa loo baahan yahay. |
| **MLX** | Moodooyinka ku habboon Apple Silicon. | MLX engine iyo madal la taageeray. |

Hubku hadda waa biinanka **LFM3-1.7B** sida LiquidAI talobixinteeda qaabaynta ku haboon, oo leh Q4_K_M, Q6_K iyo Q8_0 kala duwanaansho. Qaabka ugu horreeya ee orodku wuxuu ahaanayaa kan ku taxan qaabaynta la hagayo ee sare.

Ka sifee soo dejinta miisaanka **hoos 1 GB ilaa 500 GB iyo wixii ka sareeya**, ka dibna u kala sooc qalab ku habboon, cabbirka, soo-dhawaynta ama soo dejinta. Natiijooyinka si tartiib tartiib ah ayey u korayaan; filtarrada ayaa lagu dabaqaa moodooyinka la keenay iyo kala duwanaanshahooda caadiga ah. Talada xasuustu waxay isticmaashaa RAM/VRAM, macnaha guud iyo qaybaha wehelka, iyada oo aan laba-tirin la wadaagin xusuusta GPU.

AWQ 4-bit, GPTQ 4-bit, NF4 iyo INT8 waxay si gaar ah u soo saareen qoraal oo ku jira RTX 5070 Laptop iyagoo isticmaalaya qaab-dhismeedyo yaryar oo aan la tababarin oo si toos ah loo sameeyay. Tani waxay xaqiijinaysaa soo gelinta iyo fulinta, ma aha tayada ama waxqabadka qaab-dhismeed kasta. **Kordhintaani waxay ku jiraan isha: dib u dhiska iyo ku-tiirsanaanta waafaqsan ayaa loo baahan yahay; Cusboonaysiintan README ma cusboonaysiiso binary rakiban.**

**Lagu qiyaasay RTX 5070 Laptop:** Qwen2.5-0.5B-Instruct, macnaha guud 2,048, degdeg 272 calaamado, 128 calaamado la sameeyay, shan orod oo diirran: dhexdhexaadiyaha **GGUF Q4_K_M 402.7 tokens/s · EXL3 4 bpw 312.5 · EXL2 4 bpw 146.9** Wax faa'iido xawaare ka badan GGUF laguma muujin halkan. Tiro-samaynta/kala-soocidda way kala duwan tahay, iyo EXL3 waxay ku guul-darreysteen hal tijaabo xisaabeed oo fudud; Natiijooyinkani maahan xawaare guud ama qiimeynta tayada. [Cabbiraad buuxa iyo xaddidid](deliverables/exllama-2026-10-30/validation.md).

**TabbyAPI** waa xiriir server ah oo ikhtiyaari ah, si gaar ah loo maamulo oo ula jaanqaadaya OpenAI, oo si toos ah loo xiray. Waxaa la tijaabiyay helitaanka iyo soo-dhigga qaab-dhismeedka EXL3 ee lagu xaqiijiyay. Jidka deegaanka ee la maamulo ExLlama wuxuu iska ilaaliyaa. [ExLlama dejinta iyo TabbyAPI](transformers-server/README.md#exl3-first-exl2-second--nvidia-execution).

Nooca caadiga ah ee qalabka-la-qabsiga ah wuxuu jecel yahay sii-deynta cusub ee isla waafaqid iyo heerka xusuusta la heli karo. Moodooyinka la rakibay si toos ah looma beddelo.

[La tiriyey Safetensors dejinta](transformers-server/README.md#quantized-safetensors--source-integration-29-october-2026) · [Qaababka, mudnaanta iyo ansixinta](docs/SUIVI_MOTEURS.md)
