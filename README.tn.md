## Hub le dikai tse di tsamaelanang

**GGUF, e e tlwaelegileng le e e lekanyeditsweng Safetensors, ONNX, le MLX**: sekaseka dikai ka tiro mme o tlhophe diphetogo tse di tsamaelanang le enjene le serweboleta sa gago.

| Lelapa | Tshegetso mo motsweding | Ditlhokego |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **GGUF** | Mokwalo ka llama.cpp; dikarolo tsa setshwantsho/bidio tsa diporofaele tse di tshegediwang. | Boagi jo bo lemogiwang ke enjene e e phuthetsweng. |
| **Kgaoganya GGUF** | Mofuta o le mongwe ka motseletsele o o feletseng, bogolo jo bo akareditsweng, go laisolola ga dikarolo tsotlhe le ditlhotlhomiso tsa bogolo/SHA-256/tlhogo. | Maina a difaele a a nang le dinomoro tse di tlwaelegileng; go laisolola 500 GB ga go reye gore sekao seno se tshwanela seno PC. |
| **Tlwaelo Safetensors** | Tlatsa ditsholadifaele ka Transformers, mokwalo wa sebakwa mo CPU kgotsa NVIDIA CUDA. | Thulaganyo, tokenizer le shard nngwe le nngwe e e tlhokegang. |
| **ONNX Runtime** | Thomeloteng ya selegae ya dikerafo tsa dipalo/bool le diromelwantle tsa mafoko a a baakantsweng a GenAI. | Foldara e e feletseng e e netefaditsweng; tiro e e rileng ya go dira pele/morago ga go dira tiro e e tlhokegang; [thulaganyo le ditekanyetso](onnx-server/README.md). |
| **LoRA / PEFT** | Romelateng motheo o o feletseng o o nang le adaptara ya one e e farologaneng ya selegae mo motlameding wa Safetensors. | Motheo o o sa tlhomamisiwang wa sebakwa Transformers, boitshupo jo bo tshwanang, ditshekatsheko tsa tensor le phopholetso ya memori e e kokoantsweng; [thulaganyo le ditekanyetso](transformers-server/README.md#local-lora--peft-adapters). |
| **FP8 LLM / ModelOpt** | Tiragatso ya CUDA ya tlholego ya diromelwantle tse di kitlaneng tse di tsamaelanang Llama/Qwen2/Qwen3 ka tensara FP8. | Ditshekatsheko tsa boagi/tensor le patlisiso ya nnete ya kernel; [thulaganyo le ditekanyetso](transformers-server/README.md#modelopt-fp8-and-nvfp4-llms). |
| **NVFP4 LLM / ModelOpt** | Diponelopele tsa boloko-16 FP4 tse di pakilweng ka di-GEMM tsa CUDA tsa naga; kgaogana le setshwantsho sa NVFP4. | E tsamaelana Blackwell GPU, e tshehetswa ditekanyo/thomelontle le patlisiso ya kernel ya sebele; dikai tsa teko tse di sa katisiwang di lekilwe mo RTX 5070. |
| **AWQ** | Ditlhatlhobo tsa GEMM tsa 4-bit tse di sa kopanngwang. | NVIDIA CUDA le dilaisa tsa boikgethelo GPTQModel/Transformers. |
| **GPTQ** | Dithulaganyo tsa 2/3/4/8-bit, difomate tsa gptq/gptq_v2. | NVIDIA CUDA, GPTQModel le Optimum; tiragatso ya nnete e lekilwe mo dibiting tsa 4. |
| **bitsandbytes** | 4-bit NF4/FP4 kgotsa 8-bit INT8. | NVIDIA CUDA le ditshegetso tsa boikgethelo; diteko tsa nnete NF4 le INT8. |
| **EXL3** | Bontsi jo bo feletseng Safetensors ditsholadifaele ExLlamaV3; ya tota CUDA moloko o lekilwe. | NVIDIA GPU, tikologo e e farologaneng Python 3.12 le boagi jo bo tsamaelanang; ga go na CPU poelo morago. |
| **EXL2** | Molaisi yo o ineetseng wa ExLlamaV2; go elela, go khansela le go laisa go lekilwe mo GPU. | Tlhopha ka tlhamalalo CUDA Tlhokomelo ya morago ya mokgobo wa serweboleta se se lekilwe; lefelo la go tlhola la sekao le le feletseng le VRAM e e lekaneng e e leng teng di a tlhokega. |
| **MLX** | Dikai tse di tsamaelanang mo Apple Silicon. | MLX enjene le polatefomo e e tshegediwang. |

Jaanong Hub e a tlhoma **LFM3-1.7B** jaaka kgakololo ya yone ya LiquidAI ya dithulaganyo tse di tsamaelanang, ka diphetogo tsa Q4_K_M, Q6_K le Q8_0. Mofuta wa ntlha o o tsamayang o sala e le one o o kwadilweng mo thulaganyong e e kaelwang e e fa godimo.

Tlhotlha bokete jwa laisolola go tswa go **ka fa tlase ga 1 GB go ya go 500 GB le kwa godimo**, o bo o tlhopha ka go tshwanelega ga serweboleta, bogolo, bosheng kgotsa dilo tse di laisolotsweng. Dipholo di laisa kgato ka kgato; ditlhotlhi di dira mo dikaong tse di tserweng le mofuta wa tsone wa thulaganyetsoruri. Kgakololo ya memori e dirisa RAM/VRAM e e leng teng, dikarolo tsa bokao le tsa molekane, kwantle ga go bala gabedi memori o o abelanwang wa GPU.

AWQ 4-bit, GPTQ 4-bit, NF4 le INT8 mong le e mong hlahiswa mongolo ka RTX 5070 Laptop sebedisa dikai tse nyenyane tse sa katisoa bōpiloeng ntle le inthanete. Sena se netefatsa ho laisa le ho phethahatsa, eseng boleng kapa tshebetso ya mohlala o mong le o mong. **Ditlaleletso tse di mo motsweding: go tlhokega go aga sešwa le go ikaega go go tsamaelanang; ntšhwafatso eno ya README ga e tlhabolole bobedi jo bo tsenngwang.**

**E lekantswe mo RTX 5070 Laptop:** Qwen2.5-0.5B-Instruct, bokao 2,048, dipotso 272 dithokene, 128 dithokene tse di tlhagisitsweng, dipoelo di le tlhano tse di thuthafaditsweng: tlhaloso ya bogare **GGUF Q4_K_M 402.7 tokens/s · EXL3 4 bpw 312.5 · EXL2 4 bpw 146.9**. Ga go na mosola wa lebelo go feta GGUF o o bontshitsweng fano. Go dira dipalo/go lekanyetsa go a farologana, mme EXL3 e paletswe ke go tlhola dipalo tsa motheo; dipholo tse ga se maemo a lebelo kgotsa boleng ka kakaretso. [Ditekanyo tse di tletseng le ditekanyetso](deliverables/exllama-2026-10-30/validation.md).

**TabbyAPI** ke kgolagano ya sefara e e tsamaelanang le OpenAI e e sa patelediweng, e e laolwang ka go kgaogana, e e thibetsweng ka thulaganyetsoruri. Go ribolotswe ga sekao sa EXL3 se se netefaditsweng le go elela go ne ga lekwa. Tsela e e laolwang ya selegae ExLlama e sa ntse e farologane. [ExLlama le kgolagano ya TabbyAPI](transformers-server/README.md#exl3-first-exl2-second--nvidia-execution).

Mofuta o o tlwaelegileng wa serweboleta o rata ditokololo tse dišwa mo teng ga tier e e tshwanang ya go tsamaisana le ya memori e e leng teng. dikai hlomamisa ha ba nkeloa sebaka ka tsela e iketsang.

[Thulaganyo ya Safetensors e e lekanyeditsweng](transformers-server/README.md#quantized-safetensors--source-integration-29-october-2026) · [Difomete, ditlapele le netefatso](docs/SUIVI_MOTEURS.md)

Metswedi e e tlhabolotsweng e oketsa **dikarolo tse di laolwang tsa boikgethelo mo Dithulaganyong tsa Windows x64**: ditikologo tsa poraefete tsa Python, diporofaele tse di tsenngwang CPU/CUDA, diphopholetso le netefatso pele ga go laisolola, go netefatsa, go baakanya le go tlosa. Ditsela tsa moranodi tse di tlwaelegileng di nna ka fa tlase ga ditlhopho tsa maemo a a kwa godimo. Dienjini tsa tlholego GGUF di boloka dikarolo tsa tsone tse di leng teng; selulafouno le leru ga di laisolole Python ya desekethopo/PyTorch. Diporofaele tse tharo tsa poraefete CPU/thomelontle di ne tsa lekwa go dirisiwa tshedimosetso ya teko ya maitirelo; motlhokomedi o bega tiriso e e atlegileng ya desekethopo ka 1 Ngwanaitseele 2026; diporofaele tsa GPU le tsenyo e e phepa-PC di sa ntse di tlhoka netefatso e e farologaneng, e e tseneletseng. [Diporofaele le ditekanyetso tsa teko](docs/REPRISE_COMPOSANTS_GERES_80_LANGUES.md). Go kopanya dikarolo tseno mo EXE go a diegisiwa.
