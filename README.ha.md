## Daga farawa na farko zuwa sakamako

**Kayan aikin mu yana haɗa shirye-shiryen zuwa cikakken aikin aiwatar da aiwatarwa.** Novera yana ɗaukar matakan fasaha da ke akwai don daidaitawar da aka zaɓa, yayin da ake samun damar sarrafawa na ci gaba.

| Mataki | Abin da Novera ke sarrafa |
| --- | --- |
| **1. Fara** | Yana zaɓar harshen tsarin da aka goyan baya a farkon ƙaddamarwa kuma yana adana abin da kuke so. Yana gabatar da samfura masu farawa da bayanan kayan masarufi masu amfani. |
| **2. Shirya** | Shigarwa mai jagora yana ba da rubutu, abubuwan sakawa, hoto, kiɗa, bidiyo da haɓakawa. Yana samfotin abubuwan da aka gyara, girma da sarari kyauta kafin amincewar ku, sannan yana bin abubuwan zazzagewa tare da ci gaba, dakatarwa/ci gaba da tabbatarwa SHA-256. |
| **3. Sanya** | Suna yin rajistar samfura da abubuwan haɗinsu, suna shirya bayanan martaba masu dacewa da rahotannin abubuwan dogaro da ake buƙata. Abubuwan da aka dawo da su suna farawa akan CPU tare da adana zaɓin CPU/GPU. Sabbin bayanan martaba na kiɗa suna tsoho zuwa yanayin kayan aiki. |
| **4. Fahimtar bukatar** | Hanyoyin mota sun gane buƙatun ƙirƙirar don rubutu, hoto, kiɗa ko bidiyo. Hakanan zaka iya zaɓar yanayin fitarwa a sarari da ƙirar gida ko gajimare. |
| **5. Kashe** | Ƙwaƙwalwar ajiya ta atomatik tana jiran aiki mai aiki, tana fitar da samfuran da ba a buƙata ba kuma suna sake loda albarkatun da ake buƙata. Jerin ayyuka na 1–10 yana gudana cikin tsari kuma yana fitar da albarkatu kafin aiki na gaba. Masu samar da gajimare suna amfani da zaɓaɓɓun asusun da daidaitawa. |
| **6. Dawo da sake amfani** | Ajiye kafofin watsa labarai a cikin manyan fayilolin tsarin Novera, yana nuna sakamako da lokutan da ake da su, sannan yana ba da bambance-bambance, ɗakin karatu, kwafi, fitarwa ko raba tsarin. Aika zuwa wani aikace-aikacen ya rage aikinku. |

Automation yana rufe injunan haɗaɗɗiya da ayyukan aiki; baya sanya kowane samfurin Hub aiwatarwa. Hardware, abubuwan da aka shigar da kuma samun damar mai bada suna tantance abin da zai iya gudana. Fayiloli, canje-canjen fayil da umarnin harsashi suna bin tallafin samun damar tattaunawar.

[Jagorar mai amfani](docs/USER_GUIDE.md) · [Injiniya da inganci](docs/SUIVI_MOTEURS.md) · [Ci gaba](docs/DEVELOPMENT.md)

A cikin sabbin kafofin, **Saitunan suna daidaita shawarwarinsa zuwa wayar hannu da ƙuntataccen kayan aiki**LFM3-1.7B Q4, ko LFM3-Omni-2B Q4 tare da majiginsa lokacin da rahoton ƙwaƙwalwar ajiya da injin buƙatun sun cika. Ƙarshen gida da ba a samuwa yana ɓoye ko an toshe shi; saita hoton gajimare, kiɗa da bidiyo suna nan. Jagorar mu'amala yana sake yin amfani da kas ɗin yare na 80. Waɗannan ƙididdigar har yanzu suna buƙatar gwajin na'urar da sake ginawa don isa ga shigar da aikace-aikacen.

## Samfuran AI suna gudana cikin gida akan na'urarka

Waɗannan samfuran suna samar da tsarin jagora na yanzu. Kuna iya shigo da wasu samfura masu jituwa ko canza bayanan martaba.

| Matsayi | Model / daidaita abubuwan da aka gyara |
| --- | --- |
| **Rubutu - samfurin farko-gudu** | **Nemotron-3.5-Bolt-E4B**, fayil `Nemotron-3.5-Bolt-E4B-Q4_K_M.gguf`. |
| **Dawo da takarda** | **Qwen3-Embedding-0.6B Q8_0**, **CPU ta tsohuwa**, tare da adana zaɓi na CPU/GPU. |
| **Hoto** | **FLUX.2 Klein 4B Q4_K_M**, **Qwen3-4B Q4_K_M** encoder da hukuma FLUX.2 VAE. |
| **Hoto - zaɓi na kayan aiki** | **FLUX.2 Klein 4B NVFP4**, ta hanyar CUDA na asali lokacin da GPU da muhalli suka dace. |
| **Kida** | **ACE-Step 1.5 Turbo 2B Q4_K_M**, `acestep-5Hz-lm-1.7B-Q8_0.gguf` mai tsarawa, sadaukarwa Qwen mai rikodin kiɗa da VAE. |
| **Bidiyo** | **Wan2.1 T2V 1.3B Q4_K_M**, **UMT5-XXL Q4_K_M** encoder da Wan 2.1 VAE. |
| **Ingantawa** | Zabi **Real-ESRGAN ×2 da ×4**, tare da duban abin dogaro. |

**Duk catalogs na 80 sun rufe maɓallan Turanci na yanzu na 2,429** a fadin 15 sunaye, gami da menus, daidaitawa, saituna, taimako da sarrafa kafofin watsa labarai. Siffofin jam'i da jagorar mu'amala suna bin harshen da aka zaɓa. Amsa da harsunan murya sun dogara daban akan ƙirar.

Wannan ƙirar fasaha ba ta tabbatar da ingancin harshe ba: **bita na asali da daidaitawar yanki sun kasance wajibi**. Duba lakabin asali da sanannun iyakoki a cikin [jagorar harshe](docs/LANGUAGES.md) kuma [Rahoton bincike](deliverables/interface-audit-2026-10-29/RAPPORT.md).

## Taimakawa mutane, a kowane bangare

Bayan makaranta da ilimi, burinmu shine mu taimaka wa mutane su koyi, magance matsaloli, ƙirƙira da haɓaka ayyuka masu amfani a duk duniya. Novera yana nufin sanya ci gaban AI na yanzu cikin sauƙin fahimta da amfani, cikin harsuna, matakan ƙwarewa da kayan aikin da ake da su.

| Sassan da muke son taimakawa ci gaba | Misalai na gudunmawa mai amfani |
| --- | --- |
| Makarantu, ilimi da koyo na rayuwa | Bayyana ra'ayoyi, daidaita kayan koyo da tallafawa malamai, ɗalibai da iyalai. |
| Bincike, kimiyya da sararin samaniya | Bincika adabi, bayyana hanyoyin, tantance bayanai da raba ilimi. |
| Lafiya, kulawa da tallafin zamantakewa | Sanya bayanai cikin sauƙin fahimta da tallafawa bincike da ƙwararrun ayyukan aiki. |
| Noma, abinci da al'ummar karkara | Raba ilimi mai amfani, tallafawa tsarawa da haɓaka kayan aikin gida masu amfani. |
| Muhalli, yanayi, ruwa da halittu | Bayyana shaida, tallafawa lura da taimakawa al'ummomi tsara ayyuka. |
| Makamashi, masana'antu, gine-gine da kere-kere | Takaddun tallafi, ƙira, kiyayewa, koyan fasaha da haɓaka tsari. |
| Kasuwanci, kasuwanci, kudi da kuma aiki | Taimakawa tsara aiki, fahimtar bayanai, haɓaka ayyuka da koyon ƙwarewar sana'a. |
| Ayyukan jama'a, doka da shiga jama'a | Sanya takardu da matakai cikin sauƙin fahimta da sauƙin samun bayanai. |
| Kwamfuta, tsaro ta yanar gizo, robotics da sadarwa | Taimakawa haɓaka software, fahimtar tsarin da gina kayan aiki masu amfani. |
| Arts, al'adu, kafofin watsa labarai, harsuna da al'adun gargajiya | Taimakawa rubuce-rubuce, fassarar, hotuna, bidiyo, ayyukan ƙirƙira da raba al'adu. |
| Sufuri, dabaru, yawon shakatawa, wasanni da rayuwar al'umma | Taimakawa tare da tsarawa, sadarwa, ilmantarwa da ayyukan gida. |
| Dama, aikin jin kai da rayuwar yau da kullum | Rage shingen harshe da ilimi da tallafawa mutane tare da taimakon aiki. |

Waɗannan su ne jagororin bayar da gudummawa da ci gaba, ba wai da'awar cewa kowane fanni na musamman an riga an kawo ko ingantacce ba. Muna son jagora mai sauƙi don amfanin yau da kullun, bayyanannun zaɓuɓɓukan ci gaba lokacin da ake buƙata, kayan aiki na gaskiya da buƙatun girgije, da haɗin gwiwa tare da mutanen da suka san kowane filin da mahallin gida.
