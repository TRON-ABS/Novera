## Novera 1.0.1

Novera 1.0.1 yana samuwa don dandamalin da aka jera a ƙasa. Zazzage fakitin don tsarin ku ko amfani da tashar sabunta sa hannu. Sabunta shigarwar da ke wanzu lokacin da kuka shigar da sabon fakitin ko karɓar sabuntawa.

- Tsaro: tsauraran izini na asali don shafuka masu nisa, tabbatarwa don APIs na cibiyar sadarwa da kayan aikin uwar garken, ma'ajin sirri da aka kare da ƙaura, samun amintaccen fayil/hanyar kafofin watsa labarai, tantance maƙunsar rubutu da gina rubutun. An sabunta abubuwan dogaro da aka fallasa; sauran shawarwarin ana bin su a fili.
- Keɓantawa: ƙididdiga da rahotannin kuskure suna buƙatar izini daban kuma a kashe ta ta tsohuwa. Gina sun haɗa da ƙayyadaddun ƙayyadaddun ƙayyadaddun manufa. Ana tace gwajin fasaha; masu amfani suna kiyaye ikon kunnawa.
- Injin gida: `llama.cpp v0.9.0` (`b12110`, `cd959bae99ff4bed22bef2604f45241c09bbb8c8`). Sigar injin da sigar aikace-aikacen sun bambanta. An gyara saitunan ƙirar farko da ƙirar ƙira/masu aiki, gami da gazawar farawa da ta haɗa da `kv_unified`. Ana adana saitunan sirri masu wanzu.
- Harsuna: 24 ƙarin harsuna da bambance-bambancen suna kawo fassarorin da aka fassara tarin README zuwa 104, tare da maɓallan mu'amala na 2,435 akan kowane kasida. Wannan ɗaukar hoto baya tabbatar da ingancin edita na asali ko tallafin magana a cikin kowane harshe.
- **LFM3-Omni-2B Q4_K_M** — Default akan tebur da wayar hannu. Karamin samfurin rubutu, hotuna da sauti, ta amfani da fayil ɗin `mmproj` da ake buƙata. Zazzagewa: game da 1.39 GB na fayilolin biyu. Zaɓi shi don saitin multimodal mai sauƙi. Siffofin murya sun dogara da dandamali da injin. Tsawon mahallin yana amfani da ƙarin ƙwaƙwalwar ajiya.

Yana buƙatar ƙarin ƙwaƙwalwar ajiya fiye da LFM3-Omni kuma baya sarrafa hotuna ko sauti da kanta. Amsoshin da ake magana suna amfani da muryar tsarin. Zaɓi shi don ayyukan rubutu lokacin da kuke da isasshen ƙwaƙwalwar ajiya. Bincika amsoshi na gaskiya tare da tushe.
- Don 1.0.1, LFM3-Omni-2B Q4_K_M tare da buƙatarsa ​​`mmproj-LFM3-Omni-2B-Q4_K_M.gguf` shine tsoho shawarwarin akan tebur da wayar hannu; NVIDIA Nemotron-3.5-Bolt-E4B Q4_K_M ya kasance madadin rubutu kawai. Mahallin farko shine alamun 32,768 akan tebur da 2,048 akan wayar hannu. Dogayen mahallin suna amfani da ƙarin ƙwaƙwalwar ajiya. Zaɓuɓɓukan ƙirar da suka wanzu ana adana su har sai an canza su.

- Samfurin da aka zaɓa, ƙwaƙwalwar atomatik, haɗe-haɗe, umarni, binciken gidan yanar gizo da kayan aikin suna tsayawa a mashaya iri ɗaya. Kayan aiki sun dogara da damar samfurin kuma an ba da damar shiga.

## Daga farawa na farko zuwa sakamako

**Kayan aikin mu yana haɗa shirye-shiryen zuwa cikakken aikin aiwatar da aiwatarwa.** Novera yana ɗaukar matakan fasaha da ke akwai don daidaitawar da aka zaɓa, yayin da ake samun damar sarrafawa na ci gaba.

| --- | --- |

| **2. Shirya** | Shigarwa mai jagora yana ba da rubutu, abubuwan sakawa, hoto, kiɗa, bidiyo da haɓakawa. Yana samfotin abubuwan da aka gyara, girma da sarari kyauta kafin amincewar ku, sannan yana bin abubuwan zazzagewa tare da ci gaba, dakatarwa/ci gaba da tabbatarwa SHA-256. |

## Hub da samfura masu jituwa

**GGUF, ma'auni kuma ƙididdige Safetensors, ONNX, da MLX**: bincika samfura ta ɗawainiya kuma zaɓi bambance-bambancen da suka dace da injin da kayan aikin ku.

| Iyali | Tallafi a cikin tushe | Bukatun |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **GGUF** | Ƙirƙirar rubutu ta hanyar llama.cpp; hotuna da abubuwan bidiyo don bayanan martaba masu goyan baya. | Gine-gine da injin da aka haɗe ya gane. |
| **Raba GGUF** | Bambance-bambancen kowane cikakken jerin, girman da aka tara, zazzage duk-shard da girman/SHA-256/ duban kai. | Daidaitattun sunayen fayil masu lamba; zazzage 500 GB baya nufin samfurin ya dace da wannan PC. |
| **Daidaitaccen Safetensors** | Cikakkun manyan fayiloli ta hanyar Transformers, rubutun dalili akan CPU ko NVIDIA CUDA. | Kanfigareshan, tokenizer da kowane shard da ake buƙata. |
| **ONNX Runtime** | shigo da gida na ƙididdiga na lamba/bool da shirya fitar da rubutu GenAI. | Ingantaccen cikakken babban fayil; takamaiman aiki-takamaiman preprocessing/bayan aiki da ake buƙata; [saitin da iyaka](onnx-server/README.md). |
| **LoRA/PEFT adaftar** | Shigo da cikakken tushe tare da adaftar gida daban a cikin mai bada Safetensors. | Tushen Transformers wanda ba a ƙididdige shi ba, ainihin ma'auni, ƙididdigar tensor da ƙididdigar ƙwaƙwalwar ajiya; [saitin da iyaka](transformers-server/README.md#local-lora--peft-adapters). |
| **FP8 LLM / ModelOpt** | Kisa na asali na CUDA don fitar da Llama/Qwen2/Qwen3 mai yawa mai jituwa kowane-tensor FP8 fitarwa. | Gine-gine/tambayoyi na tensor da binciken kwaya na gaske; [saitin da iyaka](transformers-server/README.md#modelopt-fp8-and-nvfp4-llms). |
| **NVFP4 LLM / ModelOpt** | Kunshin toshe-16 FP4 tsinkaya ta asali CUDA GEMMs; daban daga hoton NVFP4. | Mai jituwa Blackwell GPU, goyan bayan girma / fitarwa da ainihin binciken kwaya; samfuran gwaji da ba a horar ba da aka gwada akan RTX 5070. |
| **AWQ** | 4-bit GEMM samfurin bincike ba tare da haɗin aiki ba. | NVIDIA CUDA da GPTQModel/Transformers na zaɓi. |
| **GPTQ** | 2/3/4/8-bit masu daidaitawa, tsarin gptq/gptq_v2. | NVIDIA CUDA, GPTQModel da Optimum; ainihin kisa da aka gwada a 4 ragowa. |
| **bitsandbytes** | 4-bit NF4/FP4 ko 8-bit INT8. | NVIDIA CUDA da abin dogaro na zaɓi; gwaje-gwaje na ainihi NF4 da INT8. |
| **EXL3** | Cikakkun manyan fayilolin Safetensors da aka ƙididdige su ta hanyar ExLlamaV3; An gwada ainihin tsara tare da CUDA. | NVIDIA GPU, ware Python 3.12 muhalli da kuma jituwa gine; Bayani na CPU |
| **EXL2** | Mai ɗaukar nauyin ExLlamaV2; yawo, sokewa da saukewa an gwada su akan GPU. | CUDA hankali dole ne a zaba a sarari don tarin software da aka gwada; ana buƙatar cikakken wurin bincike na samfur da samuwa VRAM. |
| **MLX** | Samfura masu jituwa akan Apple Silicon. | MLX engine da dandamali mai goyan baya. |

Hub yanzu yana fil **LFM3-1.7B** azaman shawararta na LiquidAI don daidaitawa masu jituwa, tare da bambance-bambancen Q4_K_M, Q6_K da Q8_0. Samfurin-gudu na farko ya kasance wanda aka jera a tsarin jagorar da ke sama.

Tace ma'aunin nauyi daga **karkashin 1 GB zuwa 500 GB da sama**, sannan a rarraba ta hanyar dacewa da hardware, girman, recency ko zazzagewa. Sakamako suna ɗauka a hankali; Ana amfani da tacewa ga samfuran da aka ɗauko da kuma bambance-bambancen da suka dace. Shawarar ƙwaƙwalwar ajiya tana amfani da samuwan RAM/VRAM, mahallin mahalli da abubuwan haɗin haɗin gwiwa, ba tare da ƙwaƙƙwaran ƙidayar GPU ba.

AWQ 4-bit, GPTQ 4-bit, NF4 da INT8 kowannen rubutu da aka kirkira akan ƙirar RTX 5070 Laptop da aka ƙirƙira ta hanyar layi. Wannan yana tabbatar da lodi da aiwatarwa, ba inganci ko aikin kowane samfuri ba. **Waɗannan ƙarin suna cikin tushen: ana buƙatar sake ginawa da dogaro masu dacewa; wannan sabuntawar README baya haɓaka aikin binary ɗin da aka shigar.**

**An auna akan RTX 5070 Laptop:** Qwen2.5-0.5B-Instruct, mahallin 2,048, ƙaddamar da alamun 272, alamun 128 da aka ƙirƙira, gudu biyar masu dumama: ƙaddamarwa na tsakiya **GGUF Q4_K_M 402.7 tokens/s · EXL3 4 bpw 312.5 · EXL2 4 bpw 146.9**. Babu fa'idar saurin gudu sama da GGUF da aka nuna anan. Ƙididdigar ƙididdigewa / daidaitawa ya bambanta, kuma EXL3 ya kasa gwajin lissafi na asali ɗaya; waɗannan sakamakon ba babban gudu ba ne ko matsayi mai inganci. [Cikakken ma'auni da iyakoki](deliverables/exllama-2026-10-30/validation.md).

**TabbyAPI** zaɓin zaɓi ne, haɗin haɗin uwar garken OpenAI mai jituwa daban, wanda aka kashe ta tsohuwa. An gwada gano samfurin EXL3 mai inganci da yawo. Hanyar ExLlama da ake gudanarwa ta kasance daban. [ExLlama saitin da haɗin TabbyAPI](transformers-server/README.md#exl3-first-exl2-second--nvidia-execution).

Tsohuwar nau'in daidaitawar kayan masarufi yana ba da fifiko ga sabbin abubuwan fitarwa tsakanin daidaito iri ɗaya da matakin ƙwaƙwalwar ajiya. Ba a maye gurbin samfuran da aka shigar ta atomatik.

[Kididdige saitin Safetensors](transformers-server/README.md#quantized-safetensors--source-integration-29-october-2026) · [Tsarukan, fifiko da inganci](docs/SUIVI_MOTEURS.md)
