Puo ea mofuta oa molumo oa lentsoe hajoale e sebelisoa ho Windows; li-platform tse ling li ntse li hloka netefatso ea matsoalloa le tšehetso e nepahetseng ea lentsoe.

[![Novera setšoantšo se hlahisitsoeng le dashboard e kopaneng e nang le bongata, peo, upscaling le mohopolo o ikemetseng](.github/assets/novera-dashboard-collapsed-2026-11-07.png)](.github/assets/novera-dashboard-collapsed-2026-11-07.png)

Phanele e le 'ngoe, mekhoa e mehlano: **auto / mongolo / setšoantšo / mmino / video**. Litaolo li latela mokhoa o khethiloeng: karo-karolelano, qeto, nako, molumo, boleng, lisebelisoa, mantsoe, peo le bongata. Bophara ba phanele le likonopo li ikamahanya le fensetere le lileibole tse fetoletsoeng.

- **1–10 meloko e latellanang**, e fetohela ho 1, ka bongata bo ikemetseng bakeng sa litšoantšo, mmino le lifitio. Emisa e hlakola mosebetsi o ntseng o sebetsa le mokoloko o setseng.
- **Ka mokhoa o sa reroang, o tsitsitseng, +1 le −1 peo**, likarohano tse seng li setiloe kapa tsa tloaelo le boikhethelo **Real-ESRGAN ×2/×4** ho nyolla ho nang le litekanyo tsa ho qetela tse bontšitsoeng.
- Mofuta o khethiloeng, memori ea othomathike, lihokelo, litaelo, patlo ea tepo le lisebelisoa li lula bareng e le 'ngoe. Lisebelisoa li ipapisitse le bokhoni ba mohlala le phihlello e fanoeng.

## Ho tloha qalong ho fihlela liphetho

**Oautomation ea rona e hokahanya boitokisetso le tšebetso e felletseng ea ts'ebetso.** Novera e sebetsana le mehato ea tekheniki e fumanehang bakeng sa litlhophiso tse khethiloeng, ha e ntse e boloka litaolo tse tsoetseng pele li fumaneha.

| Mohato | Seo Novera e sebetsanang le sona |
| --- | --- |
| **1. Qala** | E khetha puo ea sistimi e tšehetsoeng qalong ebe e boloka seo u se ratang. E fana ka mefuta ea ho qala le tlhaiso-leseling e sebetsang ea Hardware. |
| **2. Lokisetsa** | Ho kengoa ka tataiso ho fana ka mongolo, mengolo, setšoantšo, mmino, video le ho phahamisa maemo. E bona likarolo, boholo le sebaka sa mahala pele o fana ka tumello ea hau, ebe e latela tse kopilitsoeng ho latela tsoelo-pele, khefutsa/qala hape le ho netefatsa SHA-256. |
| **3. Hlophisa** | E ngolisa mefuta le likarolo tsa eona, e lokisa li-profile le litlaleho tse hlokahalang. Mecha ea ho khutlisa e qala ka CPU ka khetho e bolokiloeng ea CPU/GPU. Liprofaele tse ncha tsa 'mino li ikamahanya le maemo a liletsa. |

Phetha** | Memori e ikemetseng e emela mosebetsi o sebetsang, e lokolla mefuta e sa hlokahaleng ebe e kenya lisebelisoa tse hlokahalang hape. 1–10 letoto la mesebetsi le sebetsa ka tatellano mme le fana ka lisebelisoa pele ho mosebetsi o latelang. Bafani ba Cloud ba sebelisa akhaonto e khethiloeng le tlhophiso. |
| **6. Khutlisa 'me u sebelise hape** | E boloka mecha ea litaba ho lifoldara tsa sistimi ea Novera, e bonts'a liphetho le linako tse fumanehang, ebe e fana ka mefuta e fapaneng, laeborari, likopi, thepa e romelloang kantle kapa ho arolelana sistimi. Ho romella ts'ebeliso e 'ngoe e ntse e le ketso ea hau. |

## Hub le mefuta e lumellanang

**GGUF, e tlwaelehileng le ya palo Safetensors, ONNX, le MLX**: hlahloba mefuta ka mosebetsi ebe u khetha mefuta e fapaneng e lumellanang le enjene le lisebelisoa tsa hau.

| Lelapa | Tšehetso mohloling | Litlhoko |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **GGUF** | Mongolo ka llama.cpp; likarolo tsa setšoantšo / video bakeng sa liprofaele tse tšehetsoeng. | Meaho e amoheloang ke enjene e kopantsoeng. |
| **Arola GGUF** | Phapang e le 'ngoe ka lethathamo le felletseng, boholo bo akarelitsoeng, khoasollo ea mefuta eohle le boholo/SHA-256/litlhahlobo tsa lihlooho. | Mabitso a tloaelehileng a nang le linomoro; ho khoasolla 500 GB ha ho bolele hore mofuta ona o lekana PC. |
| **Tloaelehileng Safetensors** | Tlatsa lifoldara ka Transformers, mongolo oa causal ho CPU kapa NVIDIA CUDA. | Configuration, tokenizer le shard e 'ngoe le e 'ngoe e hlokahalang. |
| **ONNX Runtime** | Theko ea lehae ea li-graph tsa linomoro/bool le ho romelloa ka ntle ho GenAI ka mongolo. | Foldara e felletseng e netefalitsoeng; mosebetsi o khethehileng oa preprocessing/postprocessing e hlokahalang; [setup le meeli](onnx-server/README.md). |
| **LoRA / PEFT liadapter** | Fumana setsi se felletseng se nang le adaptara ea lehae e ka thoko ho mofani oa Safetensors. | Sesosa se sa lekanngoeng Transformers motheo, boitsebiso bo ts'oanang, licheke tsa tensor le khakanyo ea mohopolo o akaretsang; [setup le meeli](transformers-server/README.md#local-lora--peft-adapters). |
| **FP8 LLM / ModelOpt** | Ts'ebetso ea Setsoalle sa CUDA bakeng sa thomello e tsamaellanang e teteaneng ea Llama/Qwen2/Qwen3 per-tensor FP8. | Licheke tsa Meaho / tensor le lipatlisiso tsa kernel ea 'nete; [setup le meeli](transformers-server/README.md#modelopt-fp8-and-nvfp4-llms). |
| **NVFP4 LLM / ModelOpt** | Sebaka se pakiloeng-16 FP4 ka li-GEMM tsa khale tsa CUDA; karohano le setšoantšo NVFP4. | E lumellana Blackwell GPU, litekanyo tse tšehetsoeng / ho romela thepa le ho hlahloba kernel ea sebele; mefuta e sa rutehang ea liteko e lekiloeng ho RTX 5070. |
| **AWQ** | Li-checkpoints tsa 4-bit GEMM tse sa sebelisoang. | NVIDIA CUDA le GPTQModel/Transformers tse kenyang lisebelisoa. |
| **GPTQ** | 2/3/4/8-bit, lifomate tsa gptq/gptq_v2. | NVIDIA CUDA, GPTQModel le Optimum; ts'ebetso ea 'nete e lekiloeng ho li-bits tsa 4. |
| **bitsandbytes** | 4-bit NF4/FP4 kapa 8-bit INT8. | NVIDIA CUDA le litšepeho tsa boikhethelo; liteko tsa 'nete tsa NF4 le INT8. |
| **EXL3** | Tlatsa lifoldara tse felletseng Safetensors ka ExLlamaV3; moloko oa 'nete oa CUDA o lekoa. | NVIDIA GPU, arola Python 3.12 tikoloho le meaho e lumellanang; ha ho CPU ho khutla. |
| **EXL2** | E inehetseng ExLlamaV2 mojaro; ho lekoa ho phallela, ho hlakola le ho laolla ho GPU. | Khetha ka ho hlaka CUDA Tlhokomeliso ea morao-rao bakeng sa stack ea software e lekiloeng; ho hlokahala sebaka sa tlhahlobo se felletseng sa mohlala le VRAM e lekaneng. |
| **MLX** | Mefuta e lumellanang ho Apple Silicon. | MLX enjene le sethala se tšehetsoang. |

The Hub e se e penya **LFM3-1.7B** joalo ka likhothaletso tsa eona tsa LiquidAI bakeng sa litiisetso tse loketseng, ka Q4_K_M, Q6_K le Q8_0 mefuta e meng. Moetso oa pele o ntse o le o thathamisitsoeng ho tlhophiso e tataisitsoeng ka holimo.

Sefa ho jarolla boima ho tsoa ho **tlasa 1 GB ho leba 500 GB le ho feta**, ebe u hlopha ka hardware e lekanang, boholo, morao-rao kapa tse jarollotsoeng. Liphetho li eketseha butle-butle; Lisefe li sebetsa ho mefuta e amohetsoeng le mefuta e meng ea tsona ea kamehla. Keletso ea mohopolo e sebelisa RAM/VRAM, moelelo le likarolo tse tsamaisanang, ntle le ho bala habeli mohopolo o arolelanoeng oa GPU.

AWQ 4-bit, GPTQ 4-bit, NF4 and INT8 each generated text on an RTX 5070 Laptop using tiny untrained models created kantle ho marang-rang. Sena se tiisa ho kenya le ho sebetsa, eseng boleng kapa ts'ebetso ea mohlala o mong le o mong. **Likenyelletso tsena li mohloling: ho aha bocha le ho its'etleha ho tsamaellanang lia hlokahala; ntlafatso ena ea README ha e ntlafatse binary e kentsoeng.**

**E lekantsoe ho RTX 5070 Laptop:** Qwen2.5-0.5B-Instruct, moelelo 2,048, hang-hang 272 li-tokens, 128 li-tokens tse hlahisitsoeng, matha a mahlano a futhumatsang: li-median decode **GGUF Q4_K_M 402.7 tokens/s · EXL3 4 bpw 312.5 · EXL2 4 bpw 146.9**. Ha ho phaello ea lebelo ho feta GGUF e bontšitsoeng mona. Palo ea lipalo/calibration e ea fapana, 'me EXL3 e hlolehile ho hlahloba lipalo; liphetho tsena ha se lebelo le akaretsang kapa boemo ba boleng. [Litekanyo le meeli e felletseng](deliverables/exllama-2026-10-30/validation.md).

**TabbyAPI** ke boikhethelo, khokahano ea seva ea OpenAI e laoloang ka thoko, e koetsoeng ka ho sa feleng. Ho ile ha lekoa ho sibolloa le ho hasanya ha mofuta oa EXL3 ho netefalitsoeng. Tsela e laoloang ea sebakeng sa ExLlama e lula e fapane. [ExLlama seta le khokahanyo ea TabbyAPI](transformers-server/README.md#exl3-first-exl2-second--nvidia-execution).

Mofuta o hlophisitsoeng oa hardware o ikamahanyang le maemo o rata lintlafatso tse ncha ka har'a maemo a tšoanang le a memori a fumanehang. Mefuta e kentsoeng ha e nkeloe sebaka ka bo eona.

[tlhophiso ea Safetensors](transformers-server/README.md#quantized-safetensors--source-integration-29-october-2026) · [Mefuta, lintho tse tlang pele le netefatso](docs/SUIVI_MOTEURS.md)
